A refund agent that can’t skip steps

An orchestrator owns a finite state machine. Four FastAPI agents (classifier, policy, action, verifier) can only propose the next move. The orchestrator makes it, through a guarded transition, and high-value refunds stop for a person to approve.

    Grading hurricane damage from space

    A model that finds every building in a satellite photo taken before a disaster, compares it with the photo taken after, and grades each one: no damage, minor, major or destroyed. Built with Mulya Patel and Sakhi Patel for Advanced Topics in Machine Learning at NC State.

    The hard part is telling minor from major damage when most buildings are fine. We gave the model an explicit before-minus-after change signal, an ordinal loss that punishes far-off grades more, and weighted sampling for the rare classes.

    Overall score
    0.814, above the xView2 winner’s 0.811
    Damage F1
    0.787 across four damage grades
    Building F1
    0.875
    Stack
    PyTorch, DeepLabV3+ on ResNet-50, xBD dataset

    EZmail, an email agent

    It reads each new email, ranks it by importance and pulls out what matters: a short summary, the dates and the links. Each email becomes a card coloured by priority, so you see what needs you without opening the inbox.

    When a reply is needed, it drafts one. You choose the tone, edit the draft and send it from the same card.

    Runs as
    A containerised FastAPI microservice
    Stack
    Python, FastAPI, Docker, LLM APIs

    Recofy, music picks from your own library

    You sign in with Spotify. Recofy tags every song you’ve liked using Last.fm and OpenAI, turns the tags into embeddings and stores them in Pinecone. Recommendations are grounded in what you already listen to, not in global charts.

    Retrieval accuracy
    85%+ on the top three matches
    Stack
    Spotify API, Last.fm API, OpenAI, Pinecone
